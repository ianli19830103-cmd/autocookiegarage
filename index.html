How it works

Three steps to a masterpiece

So easy that we do it ourselves! Here is everything you need, step by step.

What you will need

Chilled sugar cookie or shortbread doughRolling pinA little flourYour car stampParchment-lined baking sheet
01

Roll

About 5 minutes

Dust your counter lightly with flour and roll the chilled dough out evenly, about a quarter inch thick.

  • Even thickness gives every cookie the same deep, clean press
  • If the dough gets sticky, chill it 15 more minutes
02

Press

About 1 minute

Cut your cookies into rounds, dust the stamp with a pinch of flour, then press straight down firmly and evenly.

  • Lift straight up and never twist, or the lines will smudge
  • Wipe crumbs off the stamp between presses for sharp details
03

Bake

Chill, then bake

Move the cookies to a parchment-lined tray and chill them 20 to 30 minutes so the car holds its shape in the oven.

  • Bake at your recipe’s usual temperature until the edges are just golden
  • Let the cookies cool on the tray before moving them

Pro tips

Keep it coldCold dough holds the sharpest lines.
Flour lightlyJust a pinch on the stamp stops sticking without hiding the detail.
Press onceOne confident press beats several tries.

Questions? We love questions!

Baking tips
What dough works best?

Sugar cookie and shortbread dough hold the sharpest lines. Chill it first.

How hard should I press?

Press firmly and evenly, straight down, then lift straight up. Twisting can smudge the lines.

Why did my cookie lose its car?

Dough that is too warm or soft spreads in the oven. Chill the stamped cookies for 20 to 30 minutes before baking.

Can I use it on other treats?

Try shortbread, sugar cookies, and other firm doughs that hold their shape.

How do I clean a stamp?

Hand wash with warm soapy water and let it dry before the next press.

About the stamps
What are the stamps made of?

Every stamp is made with PLA Pure.

What is PLA Pure?

PLA Pure is the material we use for every stamp. PLA is a plant-based plastic that is popular for 3D printing, and it holds fine details like the lines of our sports car.

Who makes the stamps?

Ian and Tal, two 10-year-old founders in San Diego, designed and tested every stamp at their kitchen table.

Is it okay for kids to use?

Yes, it is great for young bakers. Just have a grown-up handle the oven.
